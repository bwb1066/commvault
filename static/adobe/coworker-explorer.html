Discuss a starting workflow
Does selecting an application mean we have access?

No. Selections only tailor this explorer. Coworker access, underlying application licenses, supported configurations, connections, skills, and user permissions must be in place. Planned applications show potential future workflows, not current access.

Does Coworker change our permissions?

Coworker does not grant access to data or applications you are not entitled to use. Review the scope of each task and use Plan mode for proposed changes. The supported external AI-platform integrations provide read-only insights.

Are all interfaces and workflows available today?

No. The full-page workspace and in-product interfaces have different availability by application, and specific workflows depend on deployment and capabilities. B2B editions of RTCDP, AJO, and CJA are listed as coming soon. This page focuses on documented Chat workflows, not future Coworker Projects capabilities.

Does the launch price set our ongoing cost?

No. The $20,000 paid launch offer covers a separate six-month period. Ongoing licensing is a separate decision based on scope, usage, and agreed terms. The calculator illustrates Chat input consumption, not a renewal quote or a price for all Adobe workflows.

Do shorter prompts always consume fewer AI Credits?

Not under this planner's temporary 25-credit-per-Chat-input assumption. A shorter prompt does not automatically reduce that input's credit charge. Testing may help you reach useful results with fewer follow-ups; it does not guarantee savings. Model tokens and Adobe AI Credits are different measures.

Can we expand to other Adobe applications later?

Explore additional workflows as your organization adds supported applications, subject to the relevant licenses, connections, permissions, and availability. Revisit AI Credit capacity as adoption grows.
